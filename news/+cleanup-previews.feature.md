Add a Manager-only browser view ``@@cleanup-previews`` (per portal) that releases legacy ``pagePictures`` from reviews which have a usable PDF. It reports what will be cleaned and kept (with the kept objects listed), runs the cleanup with commits and instance-log progress every 1000 objects, and shows feedback afterwards. A ZEO pack is required after running it on all three portals. See ``docs/retire-preview-images.md``.
